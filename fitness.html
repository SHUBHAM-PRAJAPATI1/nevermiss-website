Questions owners ask us

Can it handle different plans and batches?

Yes. Add each membership plan or class with its price and timings in the dashboard. The bot answers from exactly what you add.

Can I turn free trials off for some classes?

Yes. Each class has a free-trial switch. The bot only offers trials where you've switched it on.

Does it reply in Hindi?

Yes. It replies in English, Hindi or Marathi, matching whatever language the person writes in.

Will it give fitness or diet advice?

No. It answers questions about your business only, and points health and diet questions to your trainers.

We're a music or dance academy, not a gym. Does it fit?

Yes. Academies get the same trial-class and joining flow, with words like classes, batches and courses instead of memberships.
