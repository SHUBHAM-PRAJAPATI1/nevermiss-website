How the 60 days run

Short, fixed check-ins, so you get the most out of the bot and we learn exactly what to improve.

  1. ApplyTwo minutes. We reply within a day.
  2. Setup call30 minutes, together. We connect your WhatsApp number and load your details.
  3. Day 1: liveWe check your bot's first replies ourselves and fix anything wrong the same day.
  4. Day 14: call 115 minutes. What works, what's wrong, what's missing. We fix your top problems within a week.
  5. Day 30: call 2We go through your numbers together and switch on bookings and reminders.
  6. Day 50: call 3Your honest review, and your partner price if you want to continue.
  7. Day 60Trial ends. Continue at the partner price, or stop. No questions asked.

What we ask in return

  • Three short calls: day 14, day 30 and day 50.
  • Honest answers: where the bot did well, where it got things wrong, and what your customers asked that it couldn't answer.
  • Optional: if you're happy with the results, a short testimonial or case study. You can always say no.

Your data stays yours

  • Your customers' data belongs to you. We never sell or share it, and we never contact your customers.
  • It is used only to run your bot and your dashboard.
  • If you leave, we delete it within 30 days on request. See our privacy policy and data deletion pages.

Questions

Is it really free for 60 days?

Yes. No card, no payment. You get the allowance above for the whole 60 days. If you want more, you can buy a small prepaid pack, but you never have to.

What happens if I use all 4,000 AI replies before the 60 days end?

We'll message you on WhatsApp when you pass 80%, and again if you run out. The bot keeps answering for a short buffer so no customer is cut off mid-chat. To keep going, add 500 replies for ₹99, or start your partner plan early: your paid period begins only after your free days end, so you lose nothing.

What happens on day 60 if I don't continue?

The bot stops replying, and nothing is charged. Your data is deleted within 30 days if you ask.

Do I need a separate WhatsApp number?

We'll help you decide on the setup call. Many businesses connect their existing business number through the official WhatsApp Business API.

Why are there only 5 spots?

We personally set up and follow every partner. Five at a time is how many we can look after properly. More batches open through the year.
