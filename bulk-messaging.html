Questions

Who can I send messages to?

Only people who agreed to receive WhatsApp messages from your business: your customers, members, patients or students who shared their number with you for updates. Promotions need their agreement to marketing too. This keeps your number in good standing with WhatsApp.

What is a template, and do I need one?

WhatsApp lets businesses message people outside an open chat only through message formats it has approved, called templates. You write it once, with blanks for things like name and date, and WhatsApp usually approves it within minutes to a few hours. Our ready ideas include the wording, and we help you get it approved.

What happens if someone replies STOP?

They're unsubscribed straight away and get a confirmation. They won't receive any campaign from you until they reply START. They can still chat with your business normally.

Do unused messages carry over?

Your plan's allowance renews every month, and unused messages from it don't carry over.

Can I send images or documents?

Text messages with buttons work today. Templates with an image, video or document at the top are coming soon.

Is there a limit on how many people I can message in a day?

WhatsApp sets a daily limit for each number, starting around 250 people and rising as your number builds a good record. NeverMiss respects it automatically and sends the rest the next day.
