Questions builders ask us

Will it quote a wrong price?

It only quotes what's in your project sheet in the dashboard. Change a price there and the next reply uses it. If a buyer asks something you haven't added, it says your team will share the exact details.

Can it work on our existing sales number?

Yes. We connect your number on the official WhatsApp Business API. Your team keeps seeing every chat in the dashboard and can take over any conversation.

We get leads from 99acres, MagicBricks and Meta ads. Does it handle those?

Any buyer who messages your WhatsApp number — from a portal, a click-to-WhatsApp ad, a hoarding or your website — is answered and qualified the same way.

Will it negotiate or promise discounts?

No. It mentions only the offers you've added. Price negotiation is always handed to your sales team.

Can our channel partners use it?

Brokers and channel partners can have their own account with their own listings and leads. Ask us about partner setups.

Where is buyer data stored?

In your private dashboard, behind your own login. We never message your buyers for anyone else. Read our privacy policy.
