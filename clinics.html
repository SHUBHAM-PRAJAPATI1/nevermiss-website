Rules it never breaks

A clinic's WhatsApp is not the place for an AI to play doctor. These limits are built in for every clinic and lab.

  • No diagnosisIt never guesses what a symptom means or whether it's serious.
  • No medicines or dosesIt never suggests a medicine, a dose or a home remedy.
  • No report readingFor labs, it never interprets values. The doctor explains reports.
  • Your charges onlyIt quotes consultation and test prices only from what you've added.
  • You confirm every slotIt takes requests and never promises a doctor is free.
Emergencies come first. If someone mentions chest pain, heavy bleeding, breathing trouble, unconsciousness or a serious injury, the first thing it says is to call 108 or go to the nearest hospital now.

Questions owners ask us

Will the bot give medical advice?

No. It's set up as a front desk, not a doctor. It won't diagnose, suggest medicines or read reports, and it tells anyone describing an emergency to call 108 or go to the nearest hospital.

Can it book an exact slot with a specific doctor?

It takes an appointment request with the preferred day, time and reason, and your staff confirms it. That way your existing appointment system stays in charge.

Where is patient data kept?

Chats and requests are stored in your dashboard, behind your clinic's own login. We never message your patients for anyone else. Read our privacy policy.

We're a lab. Can it handle home collection?

Yes. Labs get a test booking flow that asks which test, when, and whether they want home collection. It only gives fasting or preparation instructions if you've added them.

Does it work for a pet clinic?

Yes. It books vet and grooming appointments, and tells owners of a seriously unwell or injured pet to call the clinic or an emergency vet straight away.
