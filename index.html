Questions owners ask us

Will it work for my kind of business?

If your customers ask questions on WhatsApp before they book, buy or visit, yes. It has ready-made setups for coaching institutes, salons, makeup studios, gyms, yoga, dance and music studios, sports academies, dental and other clinics, labs, pet clinics, driving schools, consultants and interior designers.

Can I use my current WhatsApp number?

Usually, yes. We connect your number through Meta's official WhatsApp Business signup. On the demo call we'll check your number and tell you if a fresh one would work better.

What if the bot doesn't know an answer?

It says so plainly and offers a callback from your team, instead of guessing. It only answers from the details you add in your dashboard.

Can I see and reply to the chats myself?

Yes. Every conversation and lead is in your dashboard, and you get a WhatsApp alert for each new booking or callback request.

What happens after the free trial?

Your bot stops replying until you choose a plan. Nothing is charged automatically, because we never take a card for the trial.

Is my customers' data safe?

Chats and leads are stored in your dashboard, behind your own login. We never message your customers for anyone else. Read our privacy policy.
