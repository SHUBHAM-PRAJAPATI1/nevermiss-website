It only says what you'd say

A wrong price or a promised result costs you a client. So the bot works inside clear limits.

  • Prices from your list onlyIt never invents a price, offer or discount. If it isn't listed, it offers a callback.
  • You confirm the slotIt takes requests and never promises a time is free, so you're never double-booked.
  • No promised resultsFor colour, treatments and skin, it says your stylist will advise in person.
  • Allergies and skinIt suggests a patch test at the salon instead of giving advice.

Questions owners ask us

Will it double-book my chairs?

No. It takes appointment requests with the customer's preferred time and sends them to you. You confirm the slot, so your own calendar stays the source of truth.

Can it send my rate card and photos?

Yes, on Growth and above. Upload your rate card as a PDF and photos of your work in the dashboard, and it sends them when a customer asks.

What if a customer asks something it doesn't know?

It says it doesn't have that detail and offers a callback from you, instead of guessing.

Can I change prices and offers myself?

Yes. Edit your services and prices in the dashboard any time, and the bot uses the new ones from the next message.

Does it work for a makeup artist without a salon?

Yes. Makeup artists get a bridal and event enquiry flow that asks for the event date and city, so you can check availability and quote.
